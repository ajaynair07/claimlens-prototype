# ClaimLens prototype

**Live:** https://ajaynair07.github.io/claimlens-prototype/

ClaimLens is a claims-readiness companion for Indian health policyholders (retail and employer group cover), designed to be sold to health insurers and embedded in their app, website or WhatsApp. Before and during a hospital stay it tells the policyholder what the policy will actually pay, what could be deducted or rejected, what to submit, and where the claim stands.

Built for Assignment 2 (Option 2, build from scratch) of a product-management course, Cohort 53, by Ajay A Nair.

## What to try
- One flow, six steps: Start, Your cover, Tell us, What's paid, Prepare, Track.
- Scenario switch at the top: expecting mother (lead), older parent with an adult child managing, employee on group cover, cancer treatment.
- English or Hindi, large text, read aloud, and an **Insurer view** for the buyer.

## Notes
- Everything is synthetic: the insurer, policies, people, hospitals and amounts are fictional.
- AI outputs are simulated. Payout amounts come from a deterministic rules engine, not from AI.
- ClaimLens explains and prepares. It never decides a claim and gives no medical or legal advice.
- Single self-contained file (index.html); no tracking, no external requests.
